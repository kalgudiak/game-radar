          How to read this dashboard

          New game
          A game whose first release date falls in the chosen range. Updates, DLC and ports are left out.
          Genre
          Each game gets one main genre. When a game fits several, the most specific wins (Roguelike over Action, Puzzle over Casual). "Unclassified" means the store gave no usable genre.
          Mechanic
          Mechanics found in the store genres, tags and title, such as Merge, Runner or Idle. A game can have several.
          Traction
          Early signals: player ratings or reviews, Google Play installs, App Store chart positions, and Google Play new-game rankings by installs and trend (from AppBrain). Very new games often have none yet.
          Comparisons
          "Up" or "down" compares the chosen range with the same number of days just before it.
          Sources
          Steam (every new release), App Store (new games on the US, India, UK and Japan charts), Google Play (new games on AppBrain's 30-day new-release lists for all 17 game categories, with dates and installs from Google Play), and consoles through IGDB when its keys are set. itch.io uploads are hidden unless you tick the box, because many are prototypes, jam entries or asset packs. Updated every morning at 07:00 IST.
